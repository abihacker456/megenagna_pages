Account Deletion Request

At Megenagna, we respect your privacy and your right to control your data. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data, you can do so directly within the app, or by using the web request method below.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Fastest)

If you still have the app installed, you can delete your account immediately:

  • Open the Megenagna app.
  • Go to your Profile tab.
  • Tap on Account Settings.
  • Scroll down to the bottom and tap Delete Account.

Option 2: Web-Based Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access it, you can request account deletion via email. Please send an email from the address associated with your Megenagna account.

What Data is Deleted?

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the following:

  • Your user profile (Display Name, Username, Bio, Avatar).
  • Your authentication credentials (Email and Password).
  • All direct messages, chat history, and community messages you sent.
  • All posts, comments, and likes you created.
  • Any media files (images, videos, audio) you uploaded to our servers.

What Data is Retained?

We do not retain any personal data after an account deletion request is processed. However, please note:

  • Anonymous Analytics: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable data (e.g., total daily active users) may be retained.
  • Legal Obligations: We may retain specific transaction records (e.g., Google Play purchase receipts) if required by tax or legal regulations, but these are not linked to your active profile.

Processing Time

In-app deletions are processed immediately. Web-based email requests are processed within 7 business days. Once processed, your account cannot be recovered.
