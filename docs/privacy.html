Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

Megenagna ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: Email address and password (encrypted by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Profile Information: Display name, username, bio, and profile picture.
  • User Content: Text, images, audio, and video you upload to communities, feeds, or chats.
  • Payment Information: Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card information.

We also automatically collect certain information:

  • Device Data: Device type, operating system version, and unique device tokens (for push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Usage Data: Interactions with the app, such as likes, comments, and chat history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Megenagna app.
  • Deliver push notifications (e.g., new messages, likes, community activity).
  • Personalize your experience, including ranking posts in your Home feed using our engagement algorithm.
  • Monitor and enforce our Terms of Service, including anti-spam and abuse prevention.
  • Process premium subscription purchases.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Supabase: Used for secure authentication, database storage, and file hosting.
  • Google Play Services & Billing: Used for app distribution, crash reporting, and processing premium subscriptions.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to send push notifications to your device.
  • Google AdMob: We may display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve personalized ads.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account through the app settings, your profile and personal data will be deleted. Orphaned media files (files no longer referenced by any post or message) are automatically purged from our servers every 24 hours.

content report

We review reports of objectionable content and act within 24 hours to remove violating content and suspend offending accounts.

5. Children's Privacy

Megenagna does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You can exercise these rights directly within the app's settings or by contacting us.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including Row Level Security (RLS) on our database, to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
